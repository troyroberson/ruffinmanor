Ruffin Manor Shooting Gallery

How to Play

Grab a blaster, pick your team color, and tag the glowing targets. Every hit lands on the big scoreboard — it counts you down, keeps score, and crowns the winner.

Tonight's Games

🎯Free Play

Warm up. No clock, no pressure.

Every target is live. Blast anything you can see and rack up points for your color. Perfect for finding your aim before the real games begin.

WinJust for fun — play as long as you like.
⚡Frenzy

Beat the clock. Pure speed and chaos.

When the countdown hits GO!, fire at every target you can — each hit scores a point. Targets reset fast, so keep pulling the trigger. About two minutes of madness.

WinMost hits when time runs out takes it.
👻Haunting

Claim your territory… and hold it.

Every target you hit turns your color — you've haunted it. But rivals can steal it back by tagging it themselves, so the map keeps shifting. Guard your ground and grab theirs.

WinHaunting the most targets when the clock hits zero.
🌀Poltergeist

Chase the ghost. It won't sit still.

Only one target wakes at a time — hit it and it vanishes, then pops up somewhere else in a flash. It gets faster and faster as the 90 seconds tick down. Quick reflexes score big.

WinMost catches before the poltergeist wears you out.
